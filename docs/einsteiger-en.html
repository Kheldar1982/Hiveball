🏈 Hiveball for absolute beginners

Never played Blood Bowl, and American football isn't your thing either? No problem – here's the basic idea in two minutes, no prior knowledge needed.

What's it about?

Two teams of 5 players face off on a rectangular field. Each team has an "endzone" at its end. Goal: get the ball into the opponent's endzone. That's called a touchdown – worth one point. Whoever has scored more touchdowns at the end wins.

Getting there isn't easy: the opponent tries to knock your players down and stop them, while you fight your way to the endzone or throw the ball to a better-positioned teammate.

What does "turn-based" mean?

Unlike real football (or, say, soccer), nothing happens in real time here. It's more like chess or a board game: it's your turn, you move and act with your players one after another, then it's the opponent's turn. So you have all the time in the world to think things through.

How is it decided whether something works?

With a die (in Hiveball, a ten-sided die, "D10"). If you want to knock over an opposing player or catch the ball, for example, you roll – the better your player's stats, the higher the chance of success. So there's always a bit of luck involved, just like in real sports.

The most important terms, briefly explained

Blocking
You try to knock over an adjacent opposing player so they can't act for a moment. Can go wrong either way – your own player can fall too.
Tackle
When a player moves out of an opposing player's reach, that opponent may try to bring them down as they pass by.
Passing
The ball is thrown to a teammate instead of running it yourself – riskier, but often faster.
Touchdown
The ball reaches the opponent's endzone. Worth one point, then play resets.
Injury
A player who falls can get injured and be out for the rest of the game – similar to real contact sports.

What now?

That's enough to understand your first few turns – the rest falls into place as you play. In the game itself, the "❓ Help" button is always available with all the detailed rules. If you want the full picture, you'll find the complete rulebook at spielanleitung-en.html – it also covers all the rules for the manager part and the league.
